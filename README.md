# IntoLayers

**Turn a flat image into an editable layer stack with AI.**

[IntoLayers](https://intolayers.com/) separates a finished PNG or JPEG into transparent, editable layers. It identifies the background, text, subjects, and graphics, then preserves their original placement and stacking order so you can refine one part of an image without recreating the whole composition.

## Why IntoLayers?

Finished visuals are often difficult to reuse. A headline may need changing, a product may need replacing, or the background may need to be removed—but the original design file is gone. Manually rebuilding the artwork can take hours of selections, masks, and cleanup.

IntoLayers gives you a starting point for that edit. Upload a flat image, let the AI rebuild it as a layer stack, and continue working in the design tool you already use.

## What you get

- **Separated transparent layers** for text, subjects, graphics, and background
- **Original composition retained**, including each element's approximate position and stacking order
- **Layer preview controls** to inspect the reconstructed stack before exporting
- **Individual image downloads** when you need a single element or cutout
- **Layered PSD export** for continued work in Photoshop, Photopea, Affinity Photo, and compatible tools

## How it works

1. Visit [intolayers.com](https://intolayers.com/).
2. Upload a flat PNG or JPEG.
3. Choose **Convert**.
4. Review the generated layer stack in the browser.
5. Download the layers individually or export the complete layered PSD.

## Built for visual work that needs another pass

IntoLayers is useful when you need to make an existing image editable again:

- Update copy or translate text in a social graphic
- Reuse a campaign visual with a new product, person, or background
- Prepare image elements for motion design or animation
- Extract artwork pieces for presentations, ads, landing pages, or mockups
- Start a redesign from a screenshot or a flattened creative asset

## Pricing and availability

IntoLayers runs entirely in the browser. A limited free trial is available so you can test the result with your own image; additional conversions are available through paid credits.
